Record Format Reference

Four record shapes, source to grid. Two are stored in their source's own native format, one is computed on the fly (never persisted, still a real shape), one is the final deployed record.

Reading order: APIdetail and Demo record are the only two things actually stored, each in its own native format. vAPIstandard is a real shape — shown above — but it's built and consumed within a single pass of loadDisplayRecords, never its own persisted variable.
